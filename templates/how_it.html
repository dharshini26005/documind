🧠 Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)

Instead of generating answers from general knowledge, DocuMind Studio retrieves the most relevant content from your uploaded document using semantic search. That retrieved context is then sent to the Large Language Model (Llama 3.1 via Groq), ensuring responses are grounded in your document, reducing hallucinations and improving accuracy.
